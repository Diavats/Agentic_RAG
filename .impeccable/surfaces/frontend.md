# Surface brief: PRISM web app (frontend/)

Scope: whole app, 4 routes: / (Welcome), /chat, /how-it-works, /benchmarks.
Mode: Welcome is Persuade; Chat is Operate; How it works and Benchmarks are Read.
Audience/job/constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every answer is an album page. Five numbered pipeline slots (route, plan, retrieve, answer, verify) and the source stickers press in live as the stream arrives; an empty dashed slot honestly shows what PRISM did not find. Refuses the category default: a ChatGPT sidebar-and-bubbles chat with tiny grey source chips.

OWN-WORLD: Bubblegum pink owns the page ground (never white). Ink-black 3px outlines and hard offset shadows on everything. Stickers in yellow, lilac, mint, baby blue, slight tilt, glossy and proud of the page; empty slots are dashed ghost outlines with their number. White only inside cards, for reading. Carbon blue only for the live step.

STORY: A visitor meets Prismo, sees what PRISM reads, taps a sample question, watches the slots fill one by one with real timings, opens a source sticker to see the exact quote, and learns on How it works, via a star chart where star size is the real hybrid score, why that source won.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Welcome: a giant tilted PRISM sticker wordmark top-left over the pink ground; Prismo large at right, waving, speech bubble greeting; three sample-question stickers below as the primary action, plus one yellow "Start chatting" button; a live status pill (waking/ready) and today's quota.

FORM: Sticker Album (bolder round 2, challenger pop-culture-shelf-sticker-album-spread), with Star Atlas (notation-diagram-systems-star-atlas) as the How it works chart. Seed key bc8f2790. Code-led (no image generation).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: a sticker pressing down into its numbered slot (scale 1.06 to 1, settle, shadow shrinks) as each streamed stage lands.
